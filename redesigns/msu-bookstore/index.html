      Why shop here

      Owned by students and faculty. Not by a chain.

      MSU Bookstore has been independent since 1931. It's a 501(c)(3) Montana not-for-profit, owned and run by Montana State's faculty and students. Its one mission is to lower the cost of course materials for every student.

      How it works

      Rent

      Pay less up front and return your books by the last day of finals week. Rentals that aren't returned are charged the remaining cost plus a $10 fee.

      Rental details →

      Sell back

      Buyback runs from the Friday before finals through the last Friday of finals. Books that faculty have requested for next term are bought back at retail value.

      Buyback FAQ →

      Inclusive Access

      Digital materials are ready in Canvas on the first day of class and billed to your student account.

      How Inclusive Access works →

      Faculty adoptions

      Submit next term's materials through Verba Collect so students can see what they need and compare prices early.
