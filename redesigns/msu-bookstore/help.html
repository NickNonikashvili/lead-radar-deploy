Questions, answered.

Rentals, buyback, returns and digital materials in plain language.

Rentals

How does renting a textbook work?
It's like renting a movie. You pay a lower cost up front, use the book for the semester and return it when your class is over.
When are rentals due back?
Rented textbooks are due back the last day of finals week.
What happens if I don't return a rental?
The remaining cost of the book plus a $10 fee is charged to the card used to rent it.

Buyback

What is buyback?
Buyback is when you sell your unwanted textbooks back to the store at the end of the semester.
When does buyback happen?
From the Friday before finals week through the last Friday of finals.
Which books can I sell back?
The store buys books at retail value only when a faculty member has requested them for the upcoming semester.
What condition do books need to be in?
Normal wear and tear is fine. Books with water or liquid damage, or a lot of ripped pages, can't be accepted.

Returns

Can I return course materials?
Fall and spring course materials are refundable for the first two weeks of the term they were bought in. They must be in the same condition and come with a receipt.
What about summer courses?
Summer refund deadlines are shorter and depend on how long the course runs. Contact the store before buying if you're unsure.
Where's the full return policy?
See the returns page on msubookstore.org ↗.

Digital & Inclusive Access

What is Inclusive Access?
Inclusive Access (IA) delivers your digital textbooks and course materials in Canvas on the first day of class. The cost is charged to your university student account.
What formats are available?
Inclusive Access digital materials, access codes, eBooks, physical books and course packets.
How do I search by student ID?
Put an M in front of your student ID number in the course materials search.

Faculty

What is an adoption?
An adoption is a textbook or other course material that an instructor selects for students to use in a course.
How do I submit adoptions?
Through Verba Collect, the store's adoption partner. The course materials manager then finalizes adoptions for all Montana State courses. More for faculty →
