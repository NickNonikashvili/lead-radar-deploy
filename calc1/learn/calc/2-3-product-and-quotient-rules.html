§2.3 Product and quotient rules

Study notes for Product and quotient rules in M171 Calculus I at Montana State University: the big ideas, key formulas, a worked example, common mistakes and an exam tip. The same topic has endless practice questions and flashcards in Mathub.

Big ideas

  • Product rule: derivative of the first times the second, plus the first times derivative of the second.
  • Quotient rule: "low d-high minus high d-low, over the square of what's below." The order in the numerator matters.
  • If a quotient has a constant denominator, or the numerator is a constant times a power, simplify first: $\frac{x^3}{5} = \frac15 x^3$, $\frac{4}{x^2} = 4x^{-2}$.

Key formulas

Product rule
$$\frac{d}{dx}[f\,g] = f'g + f g'$$
Quotient rule
$$\frac{d}{dx}\left[\frac{f}{g}\right] = \frac{f'g - f g'}{g^2}$$

Worked example

Differentiate $h(x) = \dfrac{x^2 e^x}{\sin x}$ and $p(t) = t^3\cos t$.
Show the solution
$p'(t) = 3t^2\cos t - t^3\sin t$. For $h$, let the numerator be $N = x^2e^x$ with $N' = 2xe^x + x^2e^x$ (product rule). Then $h'(x) = \dfrac{(2xe^x + x^2e^x)\sin x - x^2e^x\cos x}{\sin^2 x}$.

Common mistakes

  • Writing $(fg)' = f'g'$.
  • Swapping the order in the quotient rule numerator (sign error).
Exam tipLabel f, f′, g, g′ in the margin before assembling the rule. It prevents almost every order mistake.
