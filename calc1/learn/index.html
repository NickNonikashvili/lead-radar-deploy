How MatHub works

  • Notes for every section with big ideas, formulas, a worked example, common mistakes and an exam tip.
  • Endless practice: procedurally generated questions with worked explanations, one-question lessons, daily challenges and mock exams.
  • The calendar with every homework, lab, quiz, exam and drop date from the syllabus, plus a study planner.
  • Tools: grade calculators, a GPA calculator, graphers, physics simulators, a Python playground and read-along audiobooks.
  • A class board to ask questions and study with classmates, with streaks, XP, badges and leagues to keep you going.
