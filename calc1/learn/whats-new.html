What's new in Mathub

Every release, newest first. Mathub ships most weeks; the current build is 2026.10.03.1. Ideas and bug reports are welcome on the class board or with the flag on any page.

New class: M 172 Calculus II

Sat, Oct 3, 2026 · build 2026.10.03.1
  • M 172, Calculus II, is on Mathub, built from the Fall 2026 syllabus: the trail up to each of the four exams, notes for every section from substitution to Fourier series, a formula sheet with every technique and series test, 80 flashcards and four practice exams with worked solutions.
  • Its quizzer has over a hundred kinds of questions, from u-substitution and washers to the ratio test and Taylor polynomials, with fresh numbers every time. Type exact answers like 2pi/15 or e^2-1, or a decimal.
  • Pick your lab day (Tuesday or Thursday) in the class settings and the calendar shows only your labs. WeBWorK due dates, drop deadlines and breaks are on the calendar too.

Two new classes: PSCI 230D and EMEC 100

Fri, Oct 2, 2026 · build 2026.10.02.4
  • PSCI 230D, Introduction to International Relations, is on Mathub: notes for every topic, key terms and thinkers, flashcards, an endless quizzer, and the trail up to each midterm.
  • EMEC 100, Introduction to Mechanical Engineering, is here too: topic notes, units and formulas, flashcards and a quizzer. It has no exams, so its dashboard shows when the class meets next and how it is graded.
  • Both come with the site now, so they appear for everyone as soon as it is updated.

A brighter Mathub, and Bo is the logo

Fri, Oct 2, 2026 · build 2026.10.02.3
  • The new look from this morning was too plain, so Mathub got a proper redesign: bright and friendly, in the spirit of the learning sites millions of students use, but made for Montana State. White cards on a sky-blue page, each class in its own colour, friendly bold lettering (Gabarito and Figtree), and buttons you can almost feel: they have a chunky edge and press down when you tap them.
  • Your class dashboard is now an illustrated mountain. Every section on your next exam is a stop on a winding trail up the mountain to the summit flag, coloured like ski runs by how you are doing: green circle, blue square, black diamond, or a white ring if you have not tried it yet. Bo stands at the stop you are on, with a big button to take it, and the next items on your prep checklist sit right beside it so you can tick them off.
  • Bo is the logo now: his face is in the sidebar, on the start page, in your browser tab and on your home screen if you installed Mathub. Links to Mathub you share show him too.
  • The start page has a sky-and-mountains header with Bo on the Bridger Range, and your classes are bright tiles in their own colours, each with its own little pixel picture: an integral for Calculus, Newton’s apple for Physics, a graph for Precalc, a scroll for Writing, a computer for CSCI, a potion for Biology, a heart for Kinesiology and a brain for Psychology.
  • Dark mode is a night sky with a moon over the mountains. The other looks (Realm, Bobcat, Paper, Forest, Midnight) are still in Settings → Look.

A new look: the trail map

Fri, Oct 2, 2026 · build 2026.10.02.2
  • Mathub got redesigned around one idea: every class is a mountain and its next exam is the summit. Each class dashboard now opens on a trail board: the exam, its date and how many days are left, then every section on the exam as a run up to the summit, marked green circle, blue square or black diamond by how hard that topic is for you right now (a hollow ring means you have not tried it yet). A red “You are here”, with Bo standing beside it, sits on the run to take next, and one button takes it. The board comes right under the headline, so on a laptop the whole trail and that button fit on the first screen.
  • Section notes mark every topic the same way, so you can see your hard runs from the contents.
  • The look itself: plain paper and flat map inks instead of gradients and glow, Barlow and Barlow Condensed lettering (the typeface drawn from public road signs), ruled sections instead of cards inside cards, exam reminders on trail-sign yellow, and a night map in dark mode. Bo and the pixel Bridger Range stay, now as the horizon of the start page and the ridgeline over every trail.
  • The words changed too: the dashboard says how many days are left until your exam and how to get up there, and the start page says who made Mathub and why. On your first visit the start page shows your classes first, then three steps to get going.
  • The drifting symbols in the background and geek mode are now off unless you switch them on in Settings. The other looks (Realm, Bobcat, Paper, Forest, Midnight) are still in Settings → Look.

Polish: easier to read, smoother to use

Fri, Oct 2, 2026 · build 2026.10.02.1
  • Easier to read: no label a student has to read is smaller than 11 px any more, the Physics and Precalc buttons are a shade deeper so white text on them passes contrast checks, and the exam card in dark mode uses a deep shade of each class colour instead of white on a pale one.
  • On phones the focus-sounds button moved into the top bar, so it no longer covers flashcard buttons or answer boxes, and its panel drops down from there. Messages now rise above the tab bar instead of sitting on it.
  • Smoother motion: dialogs and messages now ease out instead of vanishing, the active tab slides between tabs, and sidebar groups open and close smoothly. Every button presses in the same way. With Reduce motion on, controls still fade their colours instead of snapping.
  • Your week plays like a story: the bar at the top fills as each slide runs, numbers count up, the chart and accuracy ring draw themselves, and holding a finger on the slide pauses it.
  • Study guides and section notes show a thin reading-progress line at the top of the screen as you scroll through them.
  • Smaller fixes: “Walk me through it” is a normal button again instead of a bar across the question, “5 due” stays on one line in Today, the report flag is easier to tap, and flashcards say “Tap” on phones.

Classes without exams

Thu, Oct 1, 2026 · build 2026.10.01.4
  • Classes graded only on coursework now say so: the dashboard shows the days to your next class and how the class is graded instead of “Semester complete”, and the top bar and class card read “No exams”.

Answer letters fixed

Thu, Oct 1, 2026 · build 2026.10.01.3
  • In BIOB 160, KIN 322 and PSYX 340, questions with five choices showed “undefined” instead of the letter E in the quizzer, lessons and the daily challenge. Every choice now has its letter, and the number keys 1–6 pick answers.

New classes, without waiting for an update

Thu, Oct 1, 2026 · build 2026.10.01.2
  • Classes can now be added to Mathub straight from the admin panel, so a class you request can go live the day its syllabus arrives, with notes, practice questions, flashcards, the calendar and a grade calculator.
  • When a class you asked for goes live, your inbox and your phone tell you, with a link straight to it.
  • A Request a class link now sits in the sidebar of every class, under the class switcher.
  • New classes show up everywhere the others do: the start page, search, Today, resources, your calendar feed and reminders.

Request your class

Thu, Oct 1, 2026 · build 2026.10.01.1
  • Don’t see your class? Request it from the start page, the class picker, Settings or search. Send the course code and attach the syllabus (PDF or Word, up to 10 MB) or paste its text, and Mathub builds the class: notes, practice, the calendar and a grade calculator.
  • A Most requested list shows which classes students are asking for. Add your voice with one click; classes with the most requests and a syllabus are built first.
  • Your requests show their status (requested, being built, on Mathub), and your inbox tells you when it changes, with a link straight to the new class.
  • Syllabus files are only seen by Mathub’s administrators and are never posted. You can withdraw a request while it is new, which deletes the file. The privacy policy explains the details.

Wed, Sep 30, 2026 · build 2026.09.30.1
  • BIOB 160 Principles of Living Systems: 22 topic notes from the chemistry of life to DNA technology, every lecture and the four exams on the calendar, 86 flashcards, a formulas and key-terms sheet, exam prep, and a quizzer that generates Punnett squares, Hardy–Weinberg, pH, respiration, codon and PCR questions with worked answers.
  • KIN 322 Kinesiology: 14 topic notes from planes and axes to gait, 81 flashcards, a key-terms sheet with ROM norms, nerves and lever rules, and a quizzer with torque, muscle-force, lever, convex–concave, knee-test and gait-timing questions.   • Grade calculators for all three with the syllabus weights. KIN 322 tries all six exam-weighting options and uses the best one, as the syllabus allows.
  • Each class has its own colour, study pages under /learn, resources and a new guide on studying for biology, anatomy and psychology exams.
  • Every class calendar now lists what is coming up next to the legend, and pages with their own Print button no longer show a second one.

Simpler screens

The Realm: a fantasy tabletop look, a d20 and your character sheet

Sun, Sep 27, 2026 · build 2026.09.27.5

Body text stays Helvetica Neue; Classic is one click away in Settings → Look.

Geek mode

Sun, Sep 27, 2026 · build 2026.09.27.4
  • Page headings decode from random glyphs into their text, with a blinking cursor at the end; the breadcrumb and panel titles follow in a wave.
  • A terminal status line types the page you opened and how long it really took (for example cd calc/notes/1.1 ✓ 84 ms), and a scanline sweeps the screen on every page change and theme flip.
  • Opening a class prints a boot log of the files as they download, with their real size and time.
  • Dialogs power on like an old monitor, toasts read like shell output, dashboard tabs swap with a stepped wipe, cards materialise top-down, primary buttons shed a few bits and the logo glitches on hover.
  • There is also a code. Try the arrow keys, then B and A.
  • All of it is on by default; Settings → Preferences → Geek mode turns it off, and Reduce motion turns it off too.

Helvetica Neue

Sun, Sep 27, 2026 · build 2026.09.27.3

  • Headings are bolder and tighter to suit it; the logo wordmark stays Poppins and code stays monospace.

The Mathub logo

Sun, Sep 27, 2026 · build 2026.09.27.2

A new look, a mistakes notebook, streak freezes, calendar sync, focus together and tools for instructors

Sun, Sep 27, 2026 · build 2026.09.27.1
  • A colour system where every hue has a job: indigo for learning, green for progress, amber for rewards, coral for deadlines, teal for focus, violet for community. The floating background now carries physics, code, writing and chemistry glyphs and soft shapes, not only math.
  • Mistakes notebook: every question you miss in practice, lessons, Blitz or the daily challenge comes back with its explanation on a schedule until you get it right twice. Today shows how many are due.
  • Streak freezes: hit your daily goal on three days of a week and you earn a freeze (you can hold two); it covers one missed day on its own.
  • Calendar subscription: one private link puts every exam and deadline of your classes into Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook, updated automatically (Settings → Calendar subscription).
  • Focus together: the focus room shows who else is in a block right now, their task and time left, plus the minutes the whole site focused today.
  • Cheat-sheet builder: pick formulas, big ideas and pitfalls from any topic, add your own lines, print one page. My notes and highlights on every topic page, synced to your account.
  • Class goals: each class has a shared weekly target for questions answered; section boards on the Leagues page rank you against the people in your section.
  • Instructors and TAs can post announcements to a class, pin threads and mark a mock exam official. Invite links give both students a badge. A Sunday planning email lists next week, if you turn it on.
  • For the admin: a Growth tab with sign-ups per week, active members per day, retention by cohort and which features get used.

The big one: focus room, resources, guides, Blitz, readiness, your week, tools and motion

Sat, Sep 26, 2026 · build 2026.09.26.1
  • A Focus room: 25, 50 or 90-minute blocks with a task, sounds and a daily goal; the timer keeps running anywhere on the site and every minute counts toward your streak, quests and XP.
  • Resources: 130 curated links per class, for Montana State (tutoring, Writing Center, counseling) and for studying in general, with search, saved links that follow your account and a broken-link flag on every item.
  • Sixteen study-skills guides written for these classes: the ten-day exam plan, active recall, focus blocks, office hours, test anxiety, notes, physics problems, getting unstuck in code and more.
  • Exam readiness: one score per class from your practice, flashcards and checklist, with the three topics that raise it fastest.
  • Blitz: ninety seconds of rapid-fire questions with combo multipliers and a best score per class.
  • Your week: an animated recap of the last seven days with a share card.
  • Tools: scientific calculator, unit converter, MLA and APA citation builder, word counter with reading time, and a sig-figs helper, all offline.
  • Animations everywhere: staggered cards, ring and bar fills, a loading bar, micro-interactions, a first-visit tour, four new looks (Bobcat, Paper, Forest, Midnight) and a Reduce motion switch.
  • Print or save any notes, formula sheet, calendar or planner as a PDF.
