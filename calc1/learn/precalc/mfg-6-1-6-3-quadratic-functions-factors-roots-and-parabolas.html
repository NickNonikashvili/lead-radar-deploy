MFG 6.1–6.3 Quadratic functions: factors, roots, and parabolas

Study notes for Quadratic functions: factors, roots, and parabolas in M151Q Precalculus at Montana State University: the big ideas, key formulas, a worked example, common mistakes and an exam tip. The same topic has endless practice questions and flashcards in Mathub.

Big ideas

  • $x$-intercepts of $y = ax^2 + bx + c$ are the solutions of $ax^2 + bx + c = 0$. Factor when possible ($x^2 - 5x + 6 = (x-2)(x-3)$); otherwise complete the square or use the quadratic formula. The discriminant $b^2 - 4ac$ tells how many real roots: positive two, zero one, negative none.
  • Vertex at $x = -\dfrac{b}{2a}$ (midway between the roots); the vertex is the maximum if $a<0$, minimum if $a>0$. Vertex form $y = a(x-h)^2 + k$ shows the vertex $(h,k)$ directly.
  • Graph a parabola from its intercepts, vertex, and axis of symmetry $x = h$. Range: $[k,\infty)$ or $(-\infty,k]$.
  • Factored form $y = a(x - r_1)(x - r_2)$ is fastest when the roots are known. Applications: maximum revenue or area, projectile height.

Key formulas

Quadratic formula
$$x = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$$
Vertex
$$x_v = -\frac{b}{2a},\qquad y = a(x-h)^2 + k$$
Completing the square
$$x^2 + bx = \left(x + \tfrac b2\right)^2 - \tfrac{b^2}{4}$$

Worked example

For $y = -2x^2 + 8x + 10$, find the intercepts, vertex, and range.
Show the solution
$-2(x^2 - 4x - 5) = -2(x-5)(x+1)$: $x$-intercepts $5$ and $-1$, $y$-intercept $10$. Vertex at $x = 2$: $y = -8 + 16 + 10 = 18$, so $(2, 18)$, a maximum. Range $(-\infty, 18]$.

Common mistakes

  • Sign errors in the quadratic formula (−b, and the whole numerator over 2a).
  • Reporting the vertex x-value as the maximum value.
  • Factoring out a leading coefficient and then forgetting it.
Exam tipThe vertex is halfway between the x-intercepts. If you have the roots, you have the axis of symmetry for free.
