MatHub · resources

Free study resources for Montana State classes

Curated links for every class and for studying in general: free textbooks, video channels, practice sites, tools, and the campus services most students never use. Save any of them to your account inside MatHub.

Every class

The tools and habits that raise grades in any class: retrieval practice, spaced review, focus, and the sites everyone ends up using.

  • Khan Academy Videos
    Free video lessons and practice for every math and science topic in these classes, with mastery tracking.
  • Wolfram|Alpha Tools
    Type any math or physics question and get the answer with steps (steps need the paid tier; answers are free). Use it to check, not to skip the work.
  • Desmos graphing calculator Tools
    The fastest way to see a function, a derivative, a limit or a data set. Sliders make parameters come alive.
  • Desmos scientific calculator Tools
    A clean scientific calculator in the browser (also in MatHub Tools).
  • GeoGebra Tools
    Interactive geometry, graphing, 3D and calculus applets; thousands of ready-made demos for unit circle, derivatives and vectors.
  • Symbolab Tools
    Step-by-step solutions for algebra, calculus and trig. Good for checking a derivative or integral after you have tried it.
  • OpenStax Free books & notes
    Peer-reviewed, completely free college textbooks: University Physics, Precalculus, Calculus, Statistics and more.
  • LibreTexts Free books & notes
    A huge open library of textbooks and worked examples in math, physics, chemistry and computer science.
  • MIT OpenCourseWare Videos
    Full MIT courses with lecture videos, problem sets and exams with solutions. Single-variable calculus and classical mechanics map closely to M171 and PHSX 220.
  • Anki Apps
    The classic spaced-repetition flashcard app (free on desktop and Android). MatHub flashcards use the same idea; Anki is for building your own decks in any class.
  • Quizlet Apps
    Shared flashcard sets for almost any textbook chapter; search your class name plus the chapter.
  • Retrieval Practice Free books & notes
    The research behind why testing yourself beats rereading, with free guides for students.
  • Learning How to Learn (free course) Videos
    The most-taken online course ever: focused vs. diffuse thinking, chunking, procrastination and memory, in short videos.
  • Cornell note-taking system Free books & notes
    The two-column notes method that turns lecture notes into a self-quiz. Cornell explains it in one page.
  • Pomofocus Apps
    A minimal Pomodoro timer with tasks, if you want one outside MatHub.
  • Forest Apps
    Plant a tree that dies if you leave the app: the phone-blocking focus timer that actually works for a lot of people.
  • Lofi Girl Videos
    Endless study music streams. MatHub focus sounds and your own MP3s live in the sound bubble.
  • Google Scholar Tools
    Search academic papers and books; the "cite" button gives you MLA and APA formats instantly.
  • Zotero Apps
    Free reference manager: save sources with one click and generate a bibliography in any style.
  • ZoteroBib Tools
    Paste a URL, ISBN or DOI and get a formatted citation, no account needed.
  • Purdue OWL Reference
    The reference for MLA and APA formatting, grammar and every kind of academic writing.
  • Crash Course Videos
    Fast, funny overviews of physics, computer science, statistics and study skills.
  • Stack Exchange: Mathematics Community
    Ask a precise math question and get a careful answer; search first, most questions have been asked.
  • Physics Stack Exchange Community
    Conceptual physics questions answered by people who teach it.

At Montana State

Free help on campus that most students never use: tutoring, the Writing Center, the library, counseling and accommodations.

  • Office of Student Success Montana State
    Free tutoring, academic coaching, study-skills workshops and the people to talk to when a semester goes sideways.
  • Free tutoring (Smarty Cats) Montana State
    Drop-in and appointment tutoring for math, physics, writing and more, run through Student Success. Check the current schedule and rooms.
  • Math Learning Center Montana State
    The Math department help room for M151Q and M171: instructors and TAs on duty, no appointment. Confirm hours on the page.
  • Writing Center Montana State
    One-on-one help with any paper at any stage, in person or online. Book early before WRIT 101 deadlines.
  • MSU Library Montana State
    Study rooms you can reserve, research help chat, textbooks on reserve, and quiet floors.
  • Counseling & Psychological Services Montana State
    Free, confidential counseling for students, plus workshops on stress and test anxiety. Same-day options exist.
  • Office of Disability Services Montana State
    Accommodations such as extended exam time, note-taking support and quiet testing rooms. Register early in the semester.
  • Registrar and academic calendar Montana State
    Add/drop and withdrawal deadlines, finals schedule, registration dates. MatHub calendars mirror the class ones, but this is the source.
  • MyInfo Montana State
    Grades, registration, transcripts, financial aid and your class schedule.
  • Student Health Partners Montana State
    Medical care, dental and wellness on campus; sick notes and flu shots live here too.
  • Financial Aid Montana State
    Scholarships, emergency funds and questions about aid.
  • MSU Bookstore Montana State
    Textbook rentals and buybacks; compare with the free OpenStax and Active Calculus editions first.
  • Campus Recreation Montana State
    The fitness center, climbing wall and intramurals. A workout the day before an exam beats a fourth hour of cramming.
  • Streamline bus Montana State
    Free buses around Bozeman with live tracking.

Calculus I · M171

Active Calculus is the class text; these fill the gaps with videos, worked problems and answer checkers.

  • Active Calculus (the class text) Free books & notes
    The free, interactive textbook M171 follows, with preview activities and WeBWorK-style exercises per section.
  • Paul's Online Math Notes: Calculus I Free books & notes
    The clearest written explanations on the internet, with worked examples and practice problems that have solutions.
  • Paul's calculus cheat sheet (PDF) Reference
    Four pages of every rule and identity you will use this semester.
  • Khan Academy: Calculus 1 Videos
    Limits, derivatives, applications and integrals with exercises that give instant feedback.
  • Professor Leonard Videos
    Full-length calculus lectures that are slower and clearer than most in-person lectures. Watch at 1.5x.
  • 3Blue1Brown: Essence of Calculus Videos
    Twelve short videos that make derivatives, the chain rule and integrals feel obvious. Watch before each unit.
  • The Organic Chemistry Tutor Videos
    Dozens of worked problems per topic, exactly the kind that show up on exams.
  • PatrickJMT Videos
    Short, focused problem walkthroughs; search the channel for the exact technique.
  • blackpenredpen Videos
    Tricky limits, integrals and derivatives solved with real enthusiasm.
  • MIT 18.01 Single Variable Calculus Practice
    Complete course with problem sets and exams (with solutions) for extra exam practice.
  • Derivative Calculator Tools
    Shows the steps of a derivative, including which rule was used where. Check your homework, then close it.
  • Integral Calculator Tools
    Same idea for integrals, with the substitution or parts steps spelled out.
  • Desmos: derivative explorer Tools
    Type f(x) and then d/dx f(x) to watch the derivative graph track the slope. Great for the units 1 and 2 concept questions.

Physics I · PHSX 220

Free textbook, simulations you can push around, and the video channels that explain problems the way a good TA would.

  • OpenStax University Physics Volume 1 Free books & notes
    The free textbook for the mechanics half of the course: read the summary and try the end-of-chapter problems (answers to odds are included).
  • PhET simulations Tools
    Drag-and-play simulations for projectiles, forces, energy, momentum and pendulums. Ten minutes with one beats an hour of rereading.
  • Khan Academy: Physics Videos
    Kinematics through rotation with practice questions.
  • Flipping Physics Videos
    Every AP/intro mechanics topic as a short, careful lesson, with the common mistakes called out.
  • Michel van Biezen Videos
    Thousands of worked physics problems organized by topic; find the one that looks like your homework.
  • MIT 8.01 Classical Mechanics Practice
    Problem sets, worked solutions and exams from MIT for extra practice with a bit more depth.
  • HyperPhysics Reference
    A concept map of all of physics: click a topic, get the formulas and the connections on one page.
  • The Physics Classroom Free books & notes
    Tutorials and interactive exercises written for first-year physics, strong on free-body diagrams.
  • Isaac Physics Practice
    Free problem sets with hints that scale up in difficulty; excellent for exam-style numerical questions.
  • Crash Course Physics Videos
    Ten-minute overviews for each unit before you dive into problems.
  • Wolfram|Alpha unit conversions Tools
    Type "72 km/h in m/s" or "0.5 hp in watts". MatHub Tools has a converter that works offline.

Precalculus · M151Q

Algebra and trig refreshers, unit-circle drills, and graphing tools for functions.

  • Khan Academy: Precalculus Videos
    Functions, exponentials, logs, trig and vectors with instant-feedback practice.
  • Paul's Online Notes: Algebra and Trig Free books & notes
    Written explanations with worked examples for every algebra topic that trips people up in calculus.
  • Paul's trig cheat sheet (PDF) Reference
    Unit circle, identities, formulas: two pages to print and keep.
  • OpenStax Precalculus 2e Free books & notes
    A full free textbook with practice problems and answers.
  • Professor Leonard: Precalculus Videos
    Long, patient lectures on functions and trig.
  • Mathispower4u Videos
    Thousands of five-minute examples, one problem type each. Search "Mathispower4u" plus the topic.
  • Purplemath Free books & notes
    Plain-language algebra lessons for when a textbook explanation is not landing.
  • Math is Fun Free books & notes
    Friendly explanations with interactive diagrams for functions, logs and trig.
  • Desmos Tools
    Graph transformations, inverse functions and trig graphs with sliders.
  • GeoGebra unit circle Tools
    Interactive unit circles to drill angles, radians and the six trig values.

College Writing · WRIT 101

Citation guides, editing tools, pronunciation help and the campus Writing Center.

  • MSU Writing Center Montana State
    Free tutors for every stage of a WRIT 101 project, from brainstorming to a final polish. Book before deadline weeks fill up.
  • Purdue OWL: MLA formatting Reference
    Headers, in-text citations and the Works Cited page, with examples for every source type.
  • Purdue OWL: APA style Reference
    The same for APA 7, used by many other MSU classes.
  • ZoteroBib Tools
    Paste a link or ISBN and get the MLA or APA citation. MatHub Tools has a citation builder too.
  • Hemingway Editor Tools
    Highlights long sentences, passive voice and adverbs. Run a draft through it once, then decide what to keep.
  • Grammarly Tools
    Grammar and clarity checks in the browser and in Docs; the free tier catches most slips.
  • LanguageTool Tools
    An open-source grammar checker, good for writers whose first language is not English.
  • UNC Writing Center handouts Free books & notes
    Two-page handouts on thesis statements, introductions, transitions, revising and every essay genre.
  • Harvard: Strategies for essay writing Free books & notes
    How to outline, argue and conclude, from a college writing program.
  • Merriam-Webster Reference
    Definitions with audio pronunciations and usage notes.
  • Thesaurus.com Reference
    Find the precise word; check it in the dictionary before using it.
  • YouGlish Tools
    Hear any English word pronounced in real YouTube clips, in American or British accents. Pairs with the Phonetics Lab.
  • Forvo Tools
    Native speakers pronouncing words in hundreds of languages.
  • Project Gutenberg Free books & notes
    Seventy thousand free classic books to read for pleasure or to pull examples from.

Joy and Beauty of Data · CSCI 127

Python from the official docs down to visual debuggers, practice sites and the libraries the class uses.

  • Python documentation Reference
    The official reference. The Tutorial section is genuinely readable; the Library Reference is where you look things up.
  • The Python Tutorial Free books & notes
    Chapters 3 to 9 cover everything CSCI 127 does, in the same order.
  • Python Tutor Tools
    Paste code and step through it line by line, watching variables and the call stack. The single best tool for understanding loops, functions and lists.
  • W3Schools Python Free books & notes
    Short pages with a "Try it yourself" editor for every language feature.
  • Automate the Boring Stuff with Python Free books & notes
    A free book that teaches Python by doing useful things: files, spreadsheets, web pages.
  • Think Python (2nd edition) Free books & notes
    A free, careful introduction to programming with Python, with exercises.
  • Real Python Free books & notes
    In-depth tutorials on specific topics (f-strings, list comprehensions, classes) when the short version is not enough.
  • Corey Schafer Videos
    The best Python video tutorials for beginners: clear, well-paced, with real examples.
  • Harvard CS50's Introduction to Programming with Python Videos
    Free course with lectures and graded problem sets that line up with CSCI 127 topics.
  • Exercism: Python track Practice
    Free practice exercises with mentoring, from hello world up.
  • Codewars Practice
    Short coding challenges ranked by difficulty; the 8 kyu and 7 kyu ones fit this class.
  • Python turtle documentation Reference
    Every turtle command with examples, for the drawing assignments.
  • NumPy: the absolute basics Reference
    Arrays, indexing and vectorized math, explained for first-timers.
  • Matplotlib quick start Reference
    The figure/axes model and the plot types the class uses.
  • 10 minutes to pandas Reference
    DataFrames, selecting, filtering and grouping in one page.
  • PEP 8 style guide Reference
    How Python code is supposed to look; graders notice.
  • Thonny Apps
    A beginner-friendly Python editor with a built-in debugger that shows variables as you step.
  • VS Code Apps
    The editor most people move to; install the Python extension.
  • Replit Apps
    Code in the browser from any device, with files and packages.
  • Stack Overflow Community
    Search the exact error message in quotes. Read the accepted answer and the top comment.
  • regex101 Tools
    Test regular expressions with a live explanation of each part.

Sleep, stress, help

The unglamorous things that matter more than any study hack. If you are struggling, the people below want to hear from you.

  • 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline Wellbeing
    Call or text 988 any time, in the US, for yourself or a friend. Free and confidential.
  • Crisis Text Line Wellbeing
    Text HOME to 741741 to reach a trained counselor.
  • MSU Counseling & Psychological Services Montana State
    Free counseling on campus, including same-day and group options.
  • Sleep Foundation: students Free books & notes
    Why the all-nighter is the worst possible exam strategy, and what to do instead.
  • Headspace Apps
    Guided meditation and focus music; the basics are free.
  • Insight Timer Apps
    Thousands of free guided meditations, including short ones for before an exam.
