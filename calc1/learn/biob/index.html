BIOB 160 Principles of Living Systems: study guide

The chemistry of life, cells and energy, then genetics from Mendel to DNA technology, topic by topic. Every topic below is a free study page; the same material lives in the Mathub app with practice, flashcards, the calendar and a grade calculator for this class.

InstructorMensur Dlakic (weeks 1–8) and Alan Dyer (weeks 8–16)
MeetsMon · Wed · Fri, 12:00–12:50 pm, Romney 306
Credits4
Topics22

Exams and major deadlines

ExamWhenCovers
Exam 1Mon Sept 21 (5-hour window)Chapters 1–5: the study of life, chemistry, water, carbon, carbohydrates, lipids, proteins and nucleic acids (not Chapter 8)
Exam 2Wed Oct 14 (5-hour window)Chapters 6–9: metabolism, ATP and enzymes, cellular respiration, cell structure and membranes
Exam 3Fri Nov 13 (5-hour window)Chapters 12–16 per the syllabus: the cell cycle, meiosis, Mendelian genetics, linkage and population genetics. The lectures before it cover 12–15 and part of 23, so ask whether Chapter 16 is included
Final examWed Dec 16 · noon–1:50 pm (location TBD)Everything: half of the exam is cumulative and half covers material since Oct 14 (cell division, genetics, DNA, gene expression and DNA technology)
