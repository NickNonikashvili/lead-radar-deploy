2.4 A tour of the cell

Study notes for A tour of the cell in BIOB 160 Principles of Living Systems at Montana State University: the big ideas, key formulas, a worked example, common mistakes and an exam tip. The same topic has endless practice questions and flashcards in Mathub.

Big ideas

  • All cells have a plasma membrane, cytosol, chromosomes and ribosomes. Prokaryotic cells (bacteria, archaea) have no nucleus or membrane-bound organelles, with DNA in a nucleoid; eukaryotic cells have a nucleus and organelles.
  • Cells stay small because the surface-area-to-volume ratio falls as size grows; a large cell cannot exchange materials fast enough.
  • The endomembrane system: the nuclear envelope, the rough ER (ribosomes, makes secreted and membrane proteins), the smooth ER (lipids, detoxification, calcium storage), the Golgi apparatus (modifies, sorts and ships), lysosomes (digestive enzymes) and vacuoles (the plant central vacuole).
  • Mitochondria (respiration) and chloroplasts (photosynthesis) have their own DNA, ribosomes and double membranes, supporting the endosymbiont theory. Peroxisomes break down fatty acids and toxins, producing H₂O₂.
  • The cytoskeleton: microtubules (tubulin; cilia, flagella, spindle), microfilaments (actin; muscle, cell shape, cleavage furrow) and intermediate filaments (tension). Outside: plant cell walls, the animal extracellular matrix, and junctions (plasmodesmata, tight junctions, desmosomes, gap junctions).

Key formulas

Surface-area-to-volume (cube of side s)
$$\dfrac{6s^2}{s^3} = \dfrac{6}{s}$$

Worked example

Trace a secreted protein such as insulin from its gene to outside the cell.
Show the solution
Gene transcribed in the nucleus → mRNA → translated by ribosomes on the rough ER → protein enters the ER, then travels in a vesicle to the Golgi for modification → packaged in a secretory vesicle → released by exocytosis at the plasma membrane.

Common mistakes

  • Saying prokaryotes have no ribosomes or DNA. They have both, just no nucleus.
  • Crediting the smooth ER with protein synthesis. Ribosomes on the rough ER make secreted proteins.
  • Forgetting that plant cells have mitochondria as well as chloroplasts.
Exam tipReading: Campbell ch. 6 (pp. 93–118). Learn organelles in pairs of structure and job, and be ready to trace the secretory pathway.
