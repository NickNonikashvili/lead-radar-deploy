About us · Our story

It started with a friend’s first visit

A friend from Lyon came to see us. We planned her route, met her at the airport, took her to a family supra in Kakheti and answered her messages at midnight when her train was late.

“Everyone should see Georgia like this.”

In 2026 we made it our job. Today we’re a team of twelve in Tbilisi, and growing, with guides and drivers across the country. We still plan every trip the way we planned hers: around the person, not a template.
