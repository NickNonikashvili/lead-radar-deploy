Practical FAQ

Something else? Ask us, we’re happy to help even before you book.

Is Georgia safe for travellers?

Yes, Georgia is generally considered safe, and Georgians are famously welcoming. Use normal city sense in Tbilisi and Batumi, and in the mountains go with someone who knows the roads and the weather.

Can I drink tap water?

In Tbilisi and most mountain areas tap water is usually fine to drink. Many travellers prefer bottled or mineral water, like Borjomi, which you’ll find everywhere.

Do I need cash?

Cards work in most cafés, shops and hotels in cities. Keep some lari in cash for villages, markets, small guesthouses and marshrutkas.

How many days do I need?

A week covers Tbilisi, Kakheti and Kazbegi at a relaxed pace. Ten to fourteen days lets you add Svaneti, Kutaisi or the Black Sea.
