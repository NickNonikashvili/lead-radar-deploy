Questions people ask

How much does a project cost?

It depends on what you need, so every project gets its own quote. Quotes are free, and you will know the price before anything is booked. Send a few lines through the quote form to get one.

How long does a project take?

Most websites take one to three weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on how much content is ready. Online stores usually take three to five weeks. Larger software is planned in stages, and the quote includes a timeline.

Do you only build websites?

No. We take on any kind of project, including software and IT work: web apps, business systems, integrations, Microsoft 365 and email. Evo, Mailtabs and StudyCat on this site are our own products.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. Larger projects can be split into two payments: half to begin and half at launch. Just mention it when you get in touch.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes a period of free support. After that you can move to monthly maintenance at any time, or handle updates yourself. We hand over everything you need either way.
