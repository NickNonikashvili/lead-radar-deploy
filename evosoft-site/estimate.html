About the estimate

Is this the final price?

No. It’s an estimate worked out from what you picked. After a short talk we send a written quote with the exact scope, timeline and price, and that’s the one that counts.

What happens after I send my design?

We read it and reply within one business day with questions or a written quote. Nothing is booked until you agree to the quote.

Why is it a range?

Details change once we talk: how much content is ready, special requests, the number of products. The range allows for that, and your written quote gives one price.

Is anything saved while I design?

No. Your design is kept only in the address of this page, so you can copy the link and come back to it. Nothing is sent until you press “Request this website”.
