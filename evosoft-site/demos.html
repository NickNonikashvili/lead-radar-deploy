Try the register

  1. On a desktop browser, sign in as the owner (hr@evo.test) or the store manager (omar.haddad@evo.test).
  2. Open /pos/register/ and choose “Use this computer as Register 1” (any of 1–4 works).
  3. Sign on as 1001, PIN 1234. Press Open till, then OK to accept the $150 starting cash.
  4. Key 840001000028 and press Enter (the Hoodie Special applies itself), or tap a Quick item. F4 adds a customer: try “Avery”.
  5. Tap a line twice → Override price; supervisor 2001, PIN 1234, approves it. Then Pay.
  6. Open /pos/display/ in another tab to watch the customer display follow the sale.

Mailtabs Unified email client

Checking…mail-demo.evosoft.solutions

Open live demoCase study

One shared login with three fictional mailboxes (Work, Personal and a side project) on a private demo mail server, holding about 60 sample emails. Every person and company in them is made up.

Mail only travels between the demo mailboxes. Real addresses are refused, and real Gmail or Outlook accounts can’t be connected.

The login is shared, so you may see other visitors’ changes. Everything resets every hour.
