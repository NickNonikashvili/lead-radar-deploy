Privacy policy

This policy explains what personal data this website collects, why, and what you can do about it. EvoSoft Solutions LLC (see Who we are) is responsible for it. For any privacy question, email contact@evosoft.solutions.

Quote requests

When you send the quote form we receive your name, email address and message and, if you add them, your phone number, company, the services and timeline you picked, and the language of the page. We need your name, email and message to reply; the rest is optional. We use these details only to answer you and prepare the quote you asked for. The request is emailed to our mailbox, and a backup copy is kept on our web server in case the email fails.

Legal basis: steps you ask us to take before a possible contract or, if you write on behalf of a company, our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b) and (f), and the equivalent grounds in the Law of Georgia on Personal Data Protection). Emails and calls to us are handled the same way.

Chat assistant

When the AI assistant is switched on, our server sends your conversation (your last messages and the assistant’s replies, up to 12 in all) and the page language to our AI provider, Anthropic, PBC, in the United States, which writes the answer. We don’t send your name, IP address or any other identifier, but whatever you type is sent as written, so please don’t include personal or sensitive details. We don’t store conversations on our server; your browser keeps the current one in the tab until you close it. When the AI is switched off, the assistant answers from built-in answers in your browser and nothing you type leaves your device. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering visitors’ questions.

Abuse protection

To stop spam and limit the cost of the assistant, our server counts requests from each IP address. It stores only a one-way hash of the address, made with a key that changes every day and is then deleted, next to a counter, and it deletes counters older than two days automatically. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the site secure.

Server logs

Like every website, our hosting provider records technical data about each request (IP address, time, page requested and browser type) to deliver the site and keep it secure, and deletes these logs after a limited period. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in keeping the site secure and working.

Demo status checks

Pages that list our live demos check whether each demo is online, so your browser contacts our demo servers (evo-demo.evosoft.solutions, mail-demo.evosoft.solutions, studycat-demo.evosoft.solutions). Like any web server, they see your IP address and browser type. No cookies are sent with these checks.

What we don’t do

We don’t use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking of any kind, and our pages load no scripts, fonts, images or embeds from other companies. We don’t sell your data, and we don’t make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Who receives it

  • Our hosting provider, Hostinger, which runs this website and our mailbox.
  • Anthropic, PBC (USA), for the chat assistant’s answers. Under its commercial terms it doesn’t use them to train its models, and it keeps them only for a limited period.
  • The provider we rent our demo servers from.
  • Public authorities, where the law requires it.

Our hosting, email and AI providers process the data only on our instructions, under data processing agreements.

Transfers outside Georgia and the EU

Anthropic is in the United States. We rely on its data processing addendum, which includes the EU standard contractual clauses, as the safeguard for these transfers. You can ask us for details.

How long we keep it

  • Quote requests: the copy on our web server is deleted automatically 12 months after you send it. We delete the email the same way, unless the request leads to a project; then we keep what the contract and Georgian tax and accounting law require.
  • Chat: nothing on our server. Your browser deletes the conversation when you close the tab.
  • Abuse-protection counters: two days.
  • Server logs: the limited period set by our hosting provider.
