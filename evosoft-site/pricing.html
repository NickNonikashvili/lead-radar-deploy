FAQ

Pricing questions, answered

Do you only build websites?

No. We take on any kind of project, including software and tech: web apps, business systems, integrations, automations and IT setup. The three products in our live demos were built in-house.

How long does a project take?

Most Basic and Standard sites take one to three weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on how much content you have ready. Premium ecommerce builds usually run three to five weeks. Custom software gets a timeline with its quote.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. For Standard, Premium and custom projects we can split payment into two installments: half to begin, half at launch. Just mention it when you reach out.

What happens after the free maintenance period?

You can move to ongoing monthly maintenance at any time, or handle updates yourself. We hand over everything you need either way.

Can I upgrade my package later?

Absolutely. Many clients start with Basic or Standard and upgrade to add ecommerce, advanced SEO or custom features once they are live and growing.
