Mailtabs

Every inbox in one place, in tabs you design.

Mailtabs is a self-hosted mail client for people with too many inboxes. A tab is a set of accounts plus optional rules, so Work shows only work mail and Receipts collects every order confirmation across every account. Everything you do goes back to the real mailbox, so your phone stays in sync.

What it does

Everything in one unified email client

Tabs you design

Combine accounts and rules like “from contains @company.com” or “subject contains receipt”.

A full mail client

Write, reply, forward, Cc/Bcc, attachments, formatting, a From picker and address suggestions.

Undo and schedule send

A grace period after Send, “tomorrow 8 AM” scheduling and an Outbox with retry.

Every folder

Sent, Drafts, Spam, Trash, Archive, your own folders and Gmail labels, with unified views.

Sign in with Google or Microsoft

OAuth for Gmail and Outlook, or app passwords that are checked before they are saved.

Encrypted at rest

Connected passwords and tokens are encrypted with a server key that never leaves your host.

Features

The details

Reading

  • Unified Inbox, Starred, Sent, Drafts, Outbox, Spam and Trash
  • Conversation view
  • Search and load older mail
  • Desktop notifications
  • Print or download the original .eml

Writing

  • Rich text with formatting and links
  • Per-account signature and sender name
  • Drafts saved to the provider’s real Drafts folder
  • Attachments up to 25 MB
  • Undo send and scheduled send

Organising

  • Select many with Shift-click ranges
  • Archive, move, spam, star and mark read in bulk
  • Undo for 10 seconds (or press z)
  • Create, rename and delete folders
  • Keyboard shortcuts for everything

The login is shared, so you may see other visitors’ changes. Everything resets every hour.
