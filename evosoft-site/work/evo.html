Evo

Workforce and store in one app.

Evo replaces a stack of separate tools: employees clock in from their phones inside a GPS geofence, HR builds the schedule and approves requests, Payroll locks and exports pay periods, and the store runs on a touch-screen register that keeps selling even when the network drops.

What it does

Everything in one workforce + point of sale

Geofenced time clock

Clock in from a phone only inside the work site radius, with breaks, notes and late or early flags.

Scheduler and approvals

Week grid, templates, open shifts and trades, with conflict warnings for time off, availability and overtime.

Payroll periods

Review every timesheet, lock the period, mark it paid and export CSV for your payroll provider.

Touch-screen register

Sales, returns, exchanges, gift cards, department charges, overrides and till X/Z reports.

Inventory and purchasing

Catalogue with cost history, stock counts, purchase orders, receiving, vendors and promotions.

Built-in controls

Separation of duties, two-step sign-in for privileged roles, hash-chained audit records and encrypted backups.

Features

The details

Workforce

  • Home dashboard with a live clock card, hours, shifts and announcements
  • GPS geofence per site: block, flag or off
  • Team schedule with notes on every shift
  • Shift trades, cover requests and open-shift pickup
  • Time off balances, accruals and a team absence calendar
  • Punch corrections and timesheet sign-off

HR & Payroll

  • Live overview: who is working, late or on break
  • Approvals inbox for time off, corrections and trades
  • Employee onboarding with single-use temporary passwords
  • Effective-dated pay rates and overtime rules
  • Pay periods: open, locked, paid, with CSV export
  • Audit log of every change with a tamper check

Store

  • Register with sign-on by ID and PIN on enrolled devices
  • Sales journal searchable by receipt, SKU, customer or PO
  • Items, pricing, shelf labels and stock ledger
  • Customers (CRM), charge accounts and gift cards
  • Stock handheld app for scanners
  • Customer display for a second screen

Try the register

  1. On a desktop browser, sign in as the owner (hr@evo.test) or the store manager (omar.haddad@evo.test).
  2. Open /pos/register/ and choose “Use this computer as Register 1” (any of 1–4 works).
  3. Sign on as 1001, PIN 1234. Press Open till, then OK to accept the $150 starting cash.
  4. Key 840001000028 and press Enter (the Hoodie Special applies itself), or tap a Quick item. F4 adds a customer: try “Avery”.
  5. Tap a line twice → Override price; supervisor 2001, PIN 1234, approves it. Then Pay.
  6. Open /pos/display/ in another tab to watch the customer display follow the sale.
