Business system · Workforce + point of sale

Evo

Workforce and store in one app.

One sign-in for time clock, scheduling, HR and payroll, plus a full point-of-sale register, inventory and back office for a campus store.

  • Geofenced time clock. Clock in from a phone only inside the work site radius, with breaks, notes and late or early flags.
  • Scheduler and approvals. Week grid, templates, open shifts and trades, with conflict warnings for time off, availability and overtime.
  • Payroll periods. Review every timesheet, lock the period, mark it paid and export CSV for your payroll provider.

Web app · Unified email client

Mailtabs

Every inbox in one place, in tabs you design.

Connect Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, Yahoo or any IMAP account and sort all of it into tabs built from accounts and rules. A complete mail client, not just a reader.

  • Tabs you design. Combine accounts and rules like “from contains @company.com” or “subject contains receipt”.
  • A full mail client. Write, reply, forward, Cc/Bcc, attachments, formatting, a From picker and address suggestions.
  • Undo and schedule send. A grace period after Send, “tomorrow 8 AM” scheduling and an Outbox with retry.

Learning platform · Study hub and community

StudyCat

A free study hub for university courses.

Notes, formula sheets, flashcards, endless practice problems, interactive tools and a moderated community for eight Montana State University courses, live at studycat.org.

  • Every class, complete. Dashboard, calendar, notes, formula sheet, flashcards, quizzer, exam prep and the syllabus rules.
  • Endless practice. Procedurally generated problems with typed or multiple-choice answers, practice or timed-exam mode.
