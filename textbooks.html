Rent

Renting is like renting a movie. You pay less up front, use the book for the semester and return it when class is over. Rentals are due back the last day of finals week. Rentals that aren't returned are charged the remaining cost plus a $10 fee, on the card used to rent them.

Rental FAQ →

Sell back

Buyback runs from the Friday before finals week through the last Friday of finals. The store buys books at retail value when a faculty member has requested them for the next term. Books need to be in good condition: normal wear is fine, water damage or lots of ripped pages are not.

Buyback FAQ →

Inclusive Access

With Inclusive Access, your digital textbooks and course materials are ready in Canvas on the first day of class. The cost is charged to your university student account.

Inclusive Access details ↗

Returns

Fall and spring course materials can be refunded during the first two weeks of the term, in the same condition and with a receipt. Summer deadlines are shorter and depend on course length, so contact the store first.

For faculty

Submit your adoptions early.

An adoption is any textbook or course material an instructor selects for a class. Instructors and department coordinators submit them through the store's partner, Verba Collect. The course materials manager then finalizes adoptions for every Montana State course. The store only buys back books that faculty have requested for the next term, so early adoptions mean students can sell back more of their books.
