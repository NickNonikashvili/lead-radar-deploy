Our mission

A campus store that works for students.

MSU Bookstore is a 501(c)(3) Montana not-for-profit corporation with one mission: lower the cost of course materials for every Montana State student.

Independent since 1931

The store has been owned by Montana State's faculty and students since 1931. It's independent of the university and isn't run by a national chain, so decisions are made on campus, for campus.

Being not-for-profit means there are no outside shareholders to pay. Earnings beyond running costs go back into the store as sales and discounts. MSU Bookstore is the only not-for-profit campus store in Montana.

More than textbooks

What started as a place to buy required textbooks has grown into a small department store. It carries course materials, Bobcat clothing and gifts, school and art supplies, health and beauty items, snacks and candy, laptops and iPads, and a large selection of general interest books.
