What we can honestly promise

Gode sends your requests to the best AI model for each job. Here is exactly what happens to your data on the way, and the one thing we can’t promise.

  • AI companies never learn who you are

    Every request goes out under Gode’s own account. We never send your name, email or account details to a model provider, so they see “Gode”, not you.

  • Personal details are removed before anything leaves

    Email addresses, phone numbers, card and bank numbers, ID numbers and passwords are swapped for placeholders like [EMAIL_1] before a request is sent. Names are only caught when you introduce them (“my name is …”); other names reach the model as written. The real values are put back into the answer on our side, and answers are checked so a model can’t echo sensitive data back.

  • Zero data retention where it’s offered

    Some providers promise not to keep your request at all. Each model below is marked. Turn on Strict privacy in Settings and Gode only uses zero-retention or self-hosted models.

  • Sensitive requests only go to zero-retention models

    When a request looks private, such as money, health, passwords or legal matters, Gode sends it only to a zero-retention model, after removing your personal details. Unless we run the model ourselves, that AI company still sees the redacted text. Private pictures, video, music and voice are different: they go to media providers that may keep them, with personal details replaced by generic words like “a person”. A follow-up such as “now make a video of it” also carries the earlier message it refers to, with the same details replaced. Turn on Strict privacy to refuse those instead. To pick a model, a classifier reads each message first (with your personal details removed); messages our own checks flag as private only go to one on our servers.

  • Your chats are encrypted

    Conversations are stored encrypted with a key that belongs to your account. Our logs never contain the text of your requests, only which model answered, what it cost and how many details were hidden.

  • Delete everything, any time

    One button in Settings permanently deletes your account, every chat and your request history from our live database. Backups made before that still contain them, encrypted, until those backups expire. We keep only a one-way fingerprint of your email address, so the welcome credit can’t be claimed twice.

  • See where every request went

    Your transparency log in Settings lists the model, provider, retention and cost of every request you have made, and which classifier read it.

What we can’t promise
Unless a request runs on a model we host ourselves, the AI company that runs the model still sees the text of that request, with your personal details removed. Zero retention means they don’t keep it, not that they never see it. Removing personal details is automatic and can miss something.
